          How to play
          
      
          Each day has 3 holes. On each hole, get from the tee to the pin one link at a time, following that course's rule. Every link is a stroke.
          
      
          Your ball moves toward the hole when a link gets you closer. When the pin is one link away, you're on the green — sink the putt by naming the pin.
          
      
          Scoring
          
      
          Par is the shortest possible chain plus two.
          
      
          
        
          Shortest chainEagle −2
          
        
          Shortest + 1Birdie −1
          
        
          Shortest + 2Par E
          
        
          Shortest + 3Bogey +1
          
        
          Shortest + 4Double bogey +2
          
        
          Shortest + 5Triple bogey +3 — you pick up
          
        
          Beat our shortestAlbatross −3
          
      
          
      
          Hazards
          
      
            
        
          • Out of bounds: each hole lists 2–3 overconnected answers. You can still use them, but each costs a 1-stroke penalty and you take a drop.
            • 
        
          • Wrong link: swing and miss — 1-stroke penalty, try again from the same spot.
            • 
        
          • Mulligan: your first wrong link each day is free.
            • 
      
          
      
          No timer. Pick answers from the list as you type. New round every day at midnight Eastern.
